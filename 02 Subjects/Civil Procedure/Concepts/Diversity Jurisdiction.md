# Diversity Jurisdiction

## Two requirements ([[28 USC 1332 - Diversity|§1332(a)]])
1. The case is between **citizens of different states**; **and**
2. The amount in controversy **exceeds $75,000**.

## Connections
- [[Supplemental Jurisdiction]]: under §1367(b), in a diversity-only case, no supplemental jurisdiction over added parties or claims that would **destroy diversity**.
- [[Removal]]: a diversity case can't be removed if any defendant is a citizen of the forum state (§1441(b)), and there is a **1-year outer limit** for removal (§1446(c)).
- [[Erie Doctrine]]: in diversity cases federal courts apply state substantive law.
