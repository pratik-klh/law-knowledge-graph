# 28 U.S.C. §1331: Federal Question

> "The district courts shall have original jurisdiction of all civil actions arising under the Constitution, laws, or treaties of the United States."

- Interpreted **more narrowly** than Article III "arising under." The federal issue must be in P's claim ([[Louisville & Nashville Railroad v. Mottley|Mottley]]).
- Doctrine: [[Federal Question Jurisdiction]] · [[Well-Pleaded Complaint Rule]] · [[Substantial Federal Question]]
