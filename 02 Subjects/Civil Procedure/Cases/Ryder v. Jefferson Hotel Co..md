# Ryder v. Jefferson Hotel Co. (1922)

> [!warning] Not current law
> Contrast with modern [[Permissive Joinder of Parties|Rule 20]].

## Holding
There must be **two separate actions**, not one, against a defendant where:
- one plaintiff's cause of action does not affect the other plaintiff;
- neither plaintiff has a legal interest (a legally recognized right, claim, title, or financial stake) in the other's claim; and
- their injuries are separate.

(Context: a husband and wife insulted by a hotel clerk could not join their claims.)
