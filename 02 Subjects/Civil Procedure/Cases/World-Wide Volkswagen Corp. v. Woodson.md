# World-Wide Volkswagen Corp. v. Woodson (1980)

## Rule
> "[T]he defendant's conduct and connection with the forum State are such that he should **reasonably anticipate being haled into court there**."

## Significance
- When examining [[Minimum Contacts]], focus on the **defendant's contacts**, so that being haled into the forum is foreseeable to D. Do not focus on P's interests or the forum's interests.
- **Minimum contacts is a threshold question.** If there are none, fairness and convenience ([[Fairness Factors]]) are irrelevant.
- Established the two prongs: **sovereignty** (contacts) and **fairness** (convenience factors).
