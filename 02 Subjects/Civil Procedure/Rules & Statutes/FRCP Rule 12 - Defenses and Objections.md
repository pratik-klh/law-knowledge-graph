# FRCP Rule 12: Defenses and Objections

D has two options for responding to a complaint: (1) a Rule 12 **motion**, or (2) an **[[Answer]]**.

## Motions
- **12(b):** defenses that can be raised by pre-answer motion, including lack of SMJ, lack of PJ, improper venue, insufficient process, insufficient service, and failure to state a claim ([[Rule 12(b)(6) Motion to Dismiss|12(b)(6)]])
- **12(e):** motion for a **more definite statement** (pleading too unclear to respond to)
- **12(f):** motion to **strike** allegations

## Timing (12(a))
- Answer within **21 days** of service (**60** if service waived)
- **14 days** after denial of a Rule 12 motion

## Waiver (12(h))

| Lack of [[Personal Jurisdiction]] | Lack of [[Subject Matter Jurisdiction]] |
