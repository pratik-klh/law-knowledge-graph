# Goodyear Dunlop Tires Operations, S.A. v. Brown (2011)

## Rules
- **[[General Jurisdiction]]** exists when a corporation's operations within a state are **so substantial and continuous** that a suit against it on dealings **distinct from** those activities is justified.
- **[[Specific Jurisdiction]]** exists **only** when the cause of action arises from, or is related to, D's activities in the forum.
  - This can occur when a non-resident D, acting outside the forum, places a product in the [[Stream of Commerce]] that causes harm within the forum.

## Significance
Draws the line between GJ and SJ, and introduces the "at home" idea for corporations.
