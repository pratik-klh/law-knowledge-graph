# Byrd Balancing Test

From [[Byrd v. Blue Ridge Rural Electric Cooperative]]. Courts weigh three prongs:
1. **State interest:** how strong is the state's interest in applying its rule? What's the policy behind it?
2. **Federal interest:** how strong is the federal interest in applying the federal rule? What's the policy behind it?
3. **Modified outcome determinative test:** is there a **strong likelihood** (not just a possibility) that the result will differ depending on which law applies?

## Balancing
- Outcome determinative **+** strong state interest = **substantive**, so state law applies.
- Not outcome determinative **+** strong federal interest = **procedural**, so federal law applies.

## When to use
When there is **no** valid FRCP or federal statute directly on point (see [[Walker v. Armco Steel Corp.]]).
