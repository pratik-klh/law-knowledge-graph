# Genuine Dispute of Material Fact

The [[Summary Judgment]] standard: "no genuine dispute as to any material fact."

- **Genuine** dispute: a **reasonable fact-finder could come out either way**.
- **Material** fact: the fact **actually matters** to the outcome of the case.

The record is viewed in the light most favorable to the **non-moving** party, with all inferences drawn in its favor.
