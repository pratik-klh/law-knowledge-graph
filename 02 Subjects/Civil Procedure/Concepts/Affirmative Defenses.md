# Affirmative Defenses

- Raised in the [[Answer]] under [[FRCP Rule 8 - General Rules of Pleading|Rule 8(c)]].
- Purpose: to give **notice** to the plaintiff.
- Must be pled with enough detail to satisfy Rule 8(a). Courts are **divided** on whether the [[Plausibility Standard]] applies.
- The FRCP says the defense "must" be in the answer. If omitted, the court may treat it as **waived**.
- At [[Summary Judgment]], D bears the burden of proof on affirmative defenses.
