# Ford Motor Co. v. Montana Eighth Judicial District Court (2021)

## Rule
> "Our most common formulation of the rule demands that the suit 'arise out of **or relate to**' the defendant's contacts with the forum."

- If the connection between D's [[Purposeful Availment]] of the state and the claim is strong enough, the requirement is met.
- P's claims can "relate to" D's forum contacts **without proof of causation**, that is, without proof that P's claims came about because of D's in-state conduct.

## Significance
Broadens [[Specific Jurisdiction]]: "relate to" is a separate path from "arise out of."
