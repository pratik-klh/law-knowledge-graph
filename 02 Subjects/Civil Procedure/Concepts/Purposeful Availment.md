# Purposeful Availment

Part of the **sovereignty prong** of [[Minimum Contacts]]: is there action by D **purposely directed** toward the specific forum?

## Factors ([[Asahi Metal Industry Co. v. Superior Court]])
- Was the product **designed** for the forum?
- Was there **advertising** in the forum?
- Are there **customer advice** facilities in the forum?
- Is there a **distribution arrangement** with an agent in the forum?

In [[Ford Motor Co. v. Montana Eighth Judicial District Court]], a strong enough connection between D's purposeful availment and the claim satisfies the "arise out of or relate to" requirement.
