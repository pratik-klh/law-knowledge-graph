# FRCP Rule 9(b): Pleading Fraud with Particularity

Allegations of **fraud** must be stated **with particularity**. This is a higher standard than [[FRCP Rule 8 - General Rules of Pleading|Rule 8(a)(2)]].

## What must be pled (per the course)
- The **false statement** that was made
- What the false statement was **about**
- **Detrimental reliance** by P: how P relied, and what harm resulted

Case: [[Swanson v. Citibank]]
