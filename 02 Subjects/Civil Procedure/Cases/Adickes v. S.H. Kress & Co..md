# Adickes v. S.H. Kress & Co. (1970)

## Rule
The party moving for SJ must show there are **no questions of fact**, even if the movant **doesn't bear that burden at trial**. If the movant meets that requirement, it's entitled to judgment as a matter of law (Rule 56(a)).

(Context: a civil-rights conspiracy claim. Kress failed to foreclose the possibility that a police officer was in the store, so SJ was improper.)
