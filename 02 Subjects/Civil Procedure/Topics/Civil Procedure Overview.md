# Civil Procedure Overview

**What the course covers:** federal civil procedure, meaning what lets a civil case be brought in federal court, how cases move through the court system, why cases end up in different courts, and why some issues go to a judge and others to a jury.

## Core building blocks
- [[Civil vs Criminal Cases]]: who the parties are, what results are possible, burden of proof
- [[Substance vs Procedure]]: rights and duties versus the process for resolving disputes about them
- [[U.S. Court System Structure]]: federal and state court hierarchies
- [[Federal Rules of Civil Procedure]]: the single rulebook for federal civil cases
