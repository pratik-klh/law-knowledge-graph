# Substantial Federal Question

From [[Grable & Sons Metal Products v. Darue Engineering|Grable]] (2005) and [[Gunn v. Minton]] (2013):
- The federal question must not only appear on the face of a [[Well-Pleaded Complaint Rule|well-pleaded complaint]]; it must also be **substantial**.
- **Substantial** = important to the **actual case** **and** to the **federal system as a whole**.

| Case | Federal issue | Substantial? |
|---|---|---|
| Grable | Federal tax notice requirement | **Yes** |
| Gunn | Patent-law question inside a state malpractice claim | **No** |

Related: [[T.B. Harms Co. v. Eliscu]]. If no federal question needs to be decided at all, there is no FQJ.
