# Guaranty Trust Co. v. York (1945)

## Rule: [[Outcome Determinative Test]]
Does it significantly affect the result of the litigation for the federal court to disregard the state law that would control in state court? If yes, the issue is **substantive** and state law applies.

(Context: a state **statute of limitations** was treated as substantive.)

Later modified by [[Byrd v. Blue Ridge Rural Electric Cooperative]].
