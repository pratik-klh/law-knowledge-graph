# Permissive Joinder of Parties (Rule 20)

Covers when **plaintiffs** can sue together, and when plaintiff(s) can sue **multiple defendants**.

## Rule 20(a)(1)(A)–(B): plaintiffs may join when
1. The claims arise out of the **[[Same Transaction or Occurrence]]** (or series); **and**
2. The claims involve a **common question of law or fact**.

(Rule 20(a)(2) applies the same test to joining defendants.)

## Contrast
[[Ryder v. Jefferson Hotel Co.]] took a much stricter approach. **It is not current law.**

## SMJ caution
In diversity-only cases, [[28 USC 1367 - Supplemental Jurisdiction|§1367(b)]] bars supplemental jurisdiction over claims against Rule 20 defendants that would destroy diversity.
