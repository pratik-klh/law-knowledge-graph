# Claiming and Waiving Privilege (Rule 26(b)(5))

## Claiming
- **Expressly** claim it, usually in writing.
- **Describe** what's withheld without revealing the protected content, with enough detail for the other side to assess the claim (a **privilege log**, or redactions with context).

## Waiver
Happens through **failure to claim**, **voluntary disclosure**, **prior agreement**, or **putting the privileged matter at issue** (e.g., malpractice suits, medical malpractice).
- **Eavesdroppers:** not a waiver if the parties weren't negligent.

## Clawback (inadvertent disclosure)
1. The producing party **notifies** recipients of the claim and its basis.
2. Recipients must promptly **sequester, return, or destroy** the material, must not use or disclose it until the court rules, must take reasonable steps to retrieve it from anyone they shared it with, and may submit it to the court **under seal**.
