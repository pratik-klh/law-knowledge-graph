# Fairness Factors

The **fairness prong** of [[Minimum Contacts]]: does the suit offend "traditional notions of fair play and substantial justice"? **No one factor is determinative.**

## Factors (from [[Asahi Metal Industry Co. v. Superior Court]])
1. **Forum state's interest**: regulating the activity, protecting its citizens, enforcing its laws, providing a forum for residents
2. **Plaintiff's interest** in convenient and effective relief: Where are the witnesses? The evidence? Where does P reside?
3. **Burden on defendant**: Where does D reside? Was suit here foreseeable?
4. Relative convenience to the parties, and whether an **alternative forum** exists
5. Desire to avoid **multiple lawsuits**
6. Any other factor bearing on the fairness of the chosen forum

> [!important]
> Only reached **after** minimum contacts are found ([[World-Wide Volkswagen Corp. v. Woodson]]).
