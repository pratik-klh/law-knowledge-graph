# Louisville & Nashville Railroad Co. v. Mottley (1908)

## Rule: [[Well-Pleaded Complaint Rule]]
- Federal law must be **essential to the plaintiff's claim**. The federal issue must appear on the face of P's complaint.
- A federal issue that appears only as part of the **defense** (even if P anticipates it in the complaint) does **not** create [[Federal Question Jurisdiction|FQJ]] under [[28 USC 1331 - Federal Question|§1331]].

## Significance
This is why statutory "arising under" (§1331) is narrower than constitutional "arising under" ([[Article III Judicial Power|Article III]]).
