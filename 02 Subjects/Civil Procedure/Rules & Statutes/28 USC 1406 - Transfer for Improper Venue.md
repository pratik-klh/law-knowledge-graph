# 28 U.S.C. §1406: Cure or Waiver of Defects

- Venue is **improper**: the court **dismisses**, or **transfers** in the interest of justice to a district where the case could have been brought.
- The **transferee court's law** applies.

See [[Transfer of Venue]]
