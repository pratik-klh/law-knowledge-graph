# Required Joinder of Parties (Rule 19)

## 19(a): parties to join **if feasible**
1. Without that party, the court **cannot accord complete relief**; **or**
2. The party has an **interest** in the litigation and, if not joined, **can't protect** it; **or**
3. The party has an **interest** and, if not joined, an existing party risks **inconsistent obligations**.

If feasible, the court joins the party, usually as a defendant, but possibly as an **involuntary plaintiff**.

## 19(b): when joinder is **infeasible**
Joinder may be infeasible because:
- The court can't get **PJ** over the party
- Adding the party would **destroy SMJ**
- Adding the party would make **venue** improper

Then the court asks whether to proceed or dismiss:
- Does the party's absence **prejudice** that party or existing parties?
- Can **protective provisions** in the judgment lessen or avoid the prejudice?
- Will a judgment without the party be **adequate**?
- Will P have another **satisfactory remedy** if the action is dismissed?
