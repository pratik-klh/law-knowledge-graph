# Celotex Corp. v. Catrett (1986)

## Rule
- A movant who **doesn't bear the burden** at trial can get SJ by **pointing to an absence of evidence** supporting the non-movant's case. It doesn't need to affirmatively disprove the claim.
- The party opposing the motion **cannot rest on mere denials**. It must bring forward **specific evidence** showing a genuine issue of material fact, as it would have to at trial, to meet its burden and defeat the motion.

(Context: asbestos wrongful-death claim; the issue was exposure to Celotex's products.)
