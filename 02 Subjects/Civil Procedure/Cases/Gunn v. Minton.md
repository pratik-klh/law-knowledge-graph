# Gunn v. Minton (2013)

## Holding
A state-law **legal malpractice** claim that raised a question of **patent law** was **not substantial** enough for federal jurisdiction. The issue mattered to the parties but not to the federal system as a whole.

Compare: [[Grable & Sons Metal Products v. Darue Engineering]] · Concept: [[Substantial Federal Question]]
