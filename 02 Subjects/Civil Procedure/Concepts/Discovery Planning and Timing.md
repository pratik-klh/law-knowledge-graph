# Discovery Planning and Timing

## Rule 26(f) conference
- Parties meet "as soon as practicable" to build a **discovery plan**: when initial disclosures are due, subjects of discovery, completion date, any limits, and any requested changes to normal procedure.
- The court then issues a **scheduling order** (Rule 16).

## Sequence
- **Rule 34** requests can't be served until **22 days** after the summons and complaint are served (the rule says "more than 21 days").
- Otherwise, any discovery method can be used **in any order**. One party's discovery doesn't make others wait.

## Duty to supplement (26(e))
Correct or supplement responses if you learn they were **materially incomplete or incorrect**.

## Signature rule (26(g))
Every disclosure, request, response, and objection must be **signed by the attorney of record**. Signing certifies, after reasonable inquiry, that it is:
- consistent with the rules and warranted by law or a non-frivolous argument;
- **not for an improper purpose** (harassment, delay, raising costs);
- not unduly burdensome or unreasonable.

**Unsigned:** the recipient need not act on it. **Improper signature without justification:** sanctions, including reasonable expenses. (Compare [[FRCP Rule 11 - Sanctions|Rule 11]].)
