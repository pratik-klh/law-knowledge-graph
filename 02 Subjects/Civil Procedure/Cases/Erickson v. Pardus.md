# Erickson v. Pardus (2007)

## Rule
"**Specific facts are not necessary**." The statement need only "give the defendant fair notice of what the... claim is and the grounds upon which it rests."

Decided just after [[Bell Atlantic Corp. v. Twombly|Twombly]]. It shows that [[Notice Pleading]] survives, especially for pro se plaintiffs.
