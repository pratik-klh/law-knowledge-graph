# Subject Matter Jurisdiction (SMJ)

**Question:** is this court empowered to hear **this kind of case**? (Compare [[Personal Jurisdiction]]: power over this defendant.)

- **State courts** = courts of **general** jurisdiction
- **Federal courts** = courts of **limited** jurisdiction, defined by [[Article III Judicial Power]] and federal statutes

## Original SMJ in federal court
1. [[Diversity Jurisdiction]]: [[28 USC 1332 - Diversity]]
2. [[Federal Question Jurisdiction]]: [[28 USC 1331 - Federal Question]]

**Concurrent jurisdiction:** state courts can also hear these cases (except in very specific situations). The question is whether federal courts can hear them too. If so, federal and state courts have concurrent jurisdiction.

## Extending SMJ
- [[Supplemental Jurisdiction]]: [[28 USC 1367 - Supplemental Jurisdiction]]
- [[Removal]]: [[28 USC 1441 - Removal]] (D moves a state case to federal court)

## Can't be waived
Lack of SMJ can **never** be waived ([[FRCP Rule 12 - Defenses and Objections|Rule 12(h)]]).
