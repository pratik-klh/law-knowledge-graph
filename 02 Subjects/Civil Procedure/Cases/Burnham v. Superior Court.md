# Burnham v. Superior Court (1990)

## Holding
[[Transient Jurisdiction]] (in-state service on a physically present D) remains a valid basis for PJ, even if D is in the state **briefly** or for reasons **unrelated** to the litigation.

## Rule
If the presence is **intentional, voluntary, and knowing**, it complies with the Constitution.

## Significance
The Pennoyer presence basis survives Shaffer. See [[Traditional Bases of Personal Jurisdiction]] and [[General Jurisdiction]].
