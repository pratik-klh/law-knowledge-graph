# 28 U.S.C. §§1446–1447: Removal Procedure

- **§1446(d):** D files in federal court and serves copies on the other parties and the state court clerk.
- **§1446(b)/(c):** file within **30 days** of learning the case is removable. Diversity cases have an outside limit of **1 year** after commencement.
- **§1447(c):** a party who thinks removal was improper moves to **remand**.
