# Pleadings

## The complaint
- The FRCP shifted from **fact pleading** to **[[Notice Pleading]]**.
- **Standard:** [[FRCP Rule 8 - General Rules of Pleading|Rule 8(a)(2)]] requires a "short and plain statement of the claim showing that the pleader is entitled to relief." For fraud, [[FRCP Rule 9(b) - Pleading Fraud|Rule 9(b)]] requires particularity.
- Evolution: [[Dioguardi v. Durning]], then [[Conley v. Gibson]] ("no set of facts"), then [[Bell Atlantic Corp. v. Twombly]] (plausibility), [[Erickson v. Pardus]], and [[Ashcroft v. Iqbal]]. See [[Plausibility Standard]].

## Defendant's response: 2 options ([[FRCP Rule 12 - Defenses and Objections|Rule 12]])
1. **Motion** under Rule 12, including [[Rule 12(b)(6) Motion to Dismiss]] (applied in [[Swanson v. Citibank]])
2. **[[Answer]]**: denials, [[Affirmative Defenses]], [[Counterclaims]], and [[Crossclaims]]
