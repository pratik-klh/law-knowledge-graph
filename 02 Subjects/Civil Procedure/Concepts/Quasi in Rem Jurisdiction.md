# Quasi in Rem Jurisdiction

Settles the **rights and obligations between persons**, but the basis for PJ is **property within the state**. The property is used as a "hook." See [[Attachment]].

- [[Pennoyer v. Neff]]: property must be attached at the outset of the suit.
- [[Shaffer v. Heitner]]: due process ([[Minimum Contacts]]) now applies. Shoe is still always satisfied where ownership is conceded but the cause of action relates to rights and duties growing out of that ownership. The property is in the forum and the claim arose from that contact.
- Compulsory counterclaim exception: see [[Counterclaims]].
