# U.S. Court System Structure

## Federal system
1. **U.S. Supreme Court**: 1 court
2. **U.S. Courts of Appeals**: 13 circuits (12 regional plus the Federal Circuit)
3. **U.S. District Courts**: 94 districts, each with a Bankruptcy Court, plus the U.S. Court of International Trade and the U.S. Court of Federal Claims

## State system
1. U.S. Supreme Court (on federal questions)
2. State Supreme Courts
3. Intermediate Appellate Courts
4. State Trial Courts

## Key distinction
- State courts are **courts of general jurisdiction**.
- Federal courts are **courts of limited jurisdiction** and need a basis in [[Subject Matter Jurisdiction]].
