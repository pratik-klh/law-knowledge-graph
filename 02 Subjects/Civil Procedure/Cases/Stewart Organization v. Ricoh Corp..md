# Stewart Organization, Inc. v. Ricoh Corp. (1988)

## Rule: state law vs a federal **statute**
1. Does the statute purport to **cover the issue** between the parties? It can be **broadly** construed (unlike the narrow FRCP reading in [[Walker v. Armco Steel Corp.|Walker]]).
2. If so, is it a **valid exercise of Congressional authority**? The outline notes the answer will essentially always be yes (Congress's power over the federal courts under Article III).
3. If valid, the **statute wins** under the **Supremacy Clause**, with no balancing.

(Context: [[28 USC 1404 - Transfer for Convenience|§1404(a)]] governed a motion to transfer based on a [[Forum Selection Clauses|forum selection clause]], despite contrary state policy.)

> [!note] Added context
> The usual statement is that Congress's power comes from Article III together with the Necessary and Proper Clause (Article I).
