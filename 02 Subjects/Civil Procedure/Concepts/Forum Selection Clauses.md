# Forum Selection Clauses

**Rule:** forum selection clauses are enforced unless "**unfair or unreasonable**" ([[The Bremen v. Zapata Off-Shore Co.]]).

A plaintiff can still ask the court not to enforce one if:
- There was **fraud or duress**
- The forum has **changed** since the agreement (e.g., a party has moved)
- There was **inequality of bargaining power**
