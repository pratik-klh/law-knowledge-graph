# Well-Pleaded Complaint Rule

From [[Louisville & Nashville Railroad v. Mottley|Mottley]].

- Federal law must be **essential to P's claim** for SMJ.
- Look at whether the federal issue appears **on the face of the complaint itself**. P's own claims must be based on federal law.
  - If the claim is based on federal law, there is FQJ.
  - If not, there is no FQJ.
- An **anticipated federal defense** doesn't count.

Part of the [[Federal Question Jurisdiction]] rule, together with [[Substantial Federal Question]].
