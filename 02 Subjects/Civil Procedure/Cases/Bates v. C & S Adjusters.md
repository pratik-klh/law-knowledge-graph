# Bates v. C & S Adjusters, Inc. (1992)

## Rule
There can be **multiple districts** where a substantial part of the events took place, so venue can be proper in more than one district under [[28 USC 1391 - Venue|§1391(b)(2)]].

(Context: a debt-collection letter forwarded to, and received in, the district where P lived was a substantial event there.)
