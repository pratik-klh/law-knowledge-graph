# FRCP Rule 15: Amending the Pleadings

Situations covered:
- Amendment **as a matter of course**
- Amending **before trial**
- Amending **during or after trial**
- Amending **after the statute of limitations** has run (relation back)
- **Supplemental** pleadings, when a new matter arises after filing

See [[Pleadings]]
