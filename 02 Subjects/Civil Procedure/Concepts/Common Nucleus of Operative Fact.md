# Common Nucleus of Operative Fact

- The test for whether claims form part of the **same Article III case or controversy** under [[28 USC 1367 - Supplemental Jurisdiction|§1367(a)]].
- From [[United Mine Workers v. Gibbs]]: if state and federal claims derive from a common nucleus of operative fact, the court has power to hear the whole case.
- Closely tracks the "[[Same Transaction or Occurrence]]" test used for compulsory [[Counterclaims]] and [[Crossclaims]].
