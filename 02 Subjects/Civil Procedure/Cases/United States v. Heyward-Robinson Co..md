# United States v. Heyward-Robinson Co. (1970)

## Rule
If a counterclaim is **compulsory** (same transaction or occurrence, judged by a **logical relationship**), there is SMJ over it through **[[Supplemental Jurisdiction]]**. No independent jurisdictional basis is needed.

See [[Counterclaims]]
