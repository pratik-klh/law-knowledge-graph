# Long-Arm Statutes

- Each state's statute specifies how much [[Personal Jurisdiction]] its courts can exercise over **out-of-state** parties.
- Applies **on top of** the [[Due Process Clause]] limit. The statute must stay within due process.
- **Federal courts follow the long-arm statute of the state in which they sit.**
- Step 1 of the [[PJ Analysis Framework]]: do the facts fall within the state PJ statute?
