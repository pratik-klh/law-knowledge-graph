# Answer

## Timing ([[FRCP Rule 12 - Defenses and Objections|Rule 12(a)]])
- With no Rule 12 motion first: **21 days** after formal service, or **60 days** if service was waived (Rule 12(a)(1)(A)).
- If a Rule 12 motion is filed and denied: **14 days** after notice of the decision (Rule 12(a)(4)).

## Contents
1. Any **12(b) defenses** D wants to raise that haven't been waived
2. D's **responses** to the allegations ([[FRCP Rule 8 - General Rules of Pleading|Rule 8(b)]])
3. Any **[[Affirmative Defenses]]** ([[FRCP Rule 8 - General Rules of Pleading|Rule 8(c)]])
4. Any **[[Counterclaims]]** against P or **[[Crossclaims]]** against co-defendants (Rule 13)
