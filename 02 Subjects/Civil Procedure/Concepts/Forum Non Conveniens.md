# Forum Non Conveniens

Like a motion to transfer venue, but it usually results in the lawsuit being **dismissed** entirely, typically when the better forum is in another country.

## Test
1. Is an **alternative forum available and adequate**?
2. Would dismissal serve the parties' **private interests** and the alternative forum's **public interests**?
   - **Private:** access to proof · ability to compel unwilling witnesses · other practical problems that make trial easy, expeditious, and inexpensive · sometimes the quality of justice in the other country
   - **Public:** is it a local controversy? · what law governs? · how difficult for a jury?
