# Due Process Clause (14th Amendment)

> "...nor shall any State deprive any person of life, liberty, or property, without due process of law."

- Interpreted to mean courts may exercise [[Personal Jurisdiction]] over parties only when it would be **fair** to do so.
- Sets the **outer limit** on PJ. State [[Long-Arm Statutes]] must stay within it.
- Two-prong constitutional analysis (after [[International Shoe Co. v. Washington]]):
  1. **Sovereignty prong:** [[Minimum Contacts]]
  2. **Fairness prong:** traditional notions of fair play and substantial justice, analyzed with the [[Fairness Factors]]
