# Personal Jurisdiction (PJ)

**Definition:** the power of a court over a defendant, so the court can enter and ultimately enforce a judgment against them. It answers: *can the case be brought in State X, or must it be brought somewhere else?*

> [!example] Hypo
> I (NY citizen) get into a car accident in Montana with Dan (Montana citizen). I sue Dan for $100,000 in federal court in S.D.N.Y. **PJ question:** Can I sue Dan in NY, or must I sue him in Montana?

## Origin
The 14th Amendment's [[Due Process Clause]]: there must be PJ for due process to be satisfied. The concern is **fairness to defendants**, who don't choose to be sued.

## Two sources of limits
![[PJ Two Sources of Law]]
1. **The Constitution**: the [[Due Process Clause]] sets the *outer limit*.
2. **State [[Long-Arm Statutes]]**: each state decides how much of that limit to use. Federal courts follow the long-arm statute of the state they sit in.

## What you need
**Notice ([[Notice and Service of Process]]) + a basis.** To decide whether a basis exists, check the **long-arm statute + due process**.

## Evolution of the doctrine
1. **Territorial era:** [[Pennoyer v. Neff]] and the [[Traditional Bases of Personal Jurisdiction]] (consent, presence, residence, property). Three types: [[In Personam Jurisdiction]], [[In Rem Jurisdiction]], [[Quasi in Rem Jurisdiction]].
2. **Modern era:** [[International Shoe Co. v. Washington]], the [[Minimum Contacts]] test.
3. **Refining minimum contacts:** [[World-Wide Volkswagen Corp. v. Woodson]] (foreseeability), [[Asahi Metal Industry Co. v. Superior Court]] ([[Purposeful Availment]], [[Stream of Commerce]], [[Fairness Factors]])
4. **General vs specific:** [[Goodyear Dunlop Tires Operations v. Brown]], [[Ford Motor Co. v. Montana Eighth Judicial District Court]]; [[General Jurisdiction]] vs [[Specific Jurisdiction]]
5. **Internet:** [[Zippo Manufacturing Co. v. Zippo Dot Com]]
6. **Property & presence:** [[Shaffer v. Heitner]] (Shoe applies to in rem and quasi in rem); [[Burnham v. Superior Court]] ([[Transient Jurisdiction]] still valid)

## Current bases for PJ
- **General jurisdiction:** consent · presence + service · residence · property · systematic and continuous activity
- **Specific jurisdiction:** isolated contact with the forum, where the cause of action arises from or relates to that contact

## Why does forum choice matter?
Home-state advantage · substantive choice of law · procedural rules may differ · enforcing a judgment
