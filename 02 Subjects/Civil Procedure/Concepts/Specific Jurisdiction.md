# Specific Jurisdiction

> [!warning] Abbreviation
> The outline uses "SJ" for both specific jurisdiction and [[Supplemental Jurisdiction]] (and summary judgment). Watch context.

**Rule:** even without enough contacts for [[General Jurisdiction]], specific jurisdiction may exist where P's cause of action **arises out of or relates to** D's (even isolated) contact with the forum.

## Elements
1. Isolated contact with the forum; **and**
2. The cause of action arises from or relates to that contact.

**Test:** [[Minimum Contacts]] decides whether due process is satisfied.

## Key cases
- [[International Shoe Co. v. Washington]]: recognized SJ
- [[Goodyear Dunlop Tires Operations v. Brown]]: SJ exists **only** when the claim arises from or relates to D's forum activities. This can include placing a product in the [[Stream of Commerce]] that causes harm in the forum.
- [[Ford Motor Co. v. Montana Eighth Judicial District Court]]: "relate to" does **not** require proof of causation
- [[Asahi Metal Industry Co. v. Superior Court]]: [[Purposeful Availment]] and [[Fairness Factors]]
