# Grable & Sons Metal Products, Inc. v. Darue Engineering & Mfg. (2005)

## Rule
The federal question must appear on the face of a [[Well-Pleaded Complaint Rule|well-pleaded complaint]] **and** be **substantial**: important to the case and to the federal system as a whole.

## Application
The federal issue (the IRS **notice requirement** in a state quiet-title action) was **substantial**, so there was FQJ.

Compare: [[Gunn v. Minton]] · Concept: [[Substantial Federal Question]]
