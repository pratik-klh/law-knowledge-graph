# Attachment

A court order to **seize or place a lien** on property (e.g., real estate). It stops the owner from selling or hiding the property, so it stays available to pay a potential debt or judgment.

- Basis for [[Quasi in Rem Jurisdiction]].
- Central to [[Pennoyer v. Neff]]: Neff's land was not attached at the start of Lawsuit #1.
