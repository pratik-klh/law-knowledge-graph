# Zippo Manufacturing Co. v. Zippo Dot Com, Inc. (1997)

## Rule: sliding scale for website contacts
| Website type | What D does | Jurisdiction |
|---|---|---|
| **Passive** | Only posts information, which can be accessed in the forum | **NO** |
| **Interactive** | Can send or receive information to and from the forum | **MAYBE** |
| **Active** | Uses the site to transmit files to the forum and contract with forum residents | **YES** |

## Significance
Applies [[Minimum Contacts]] and [[Purposeful Availment]] to internet activity.
