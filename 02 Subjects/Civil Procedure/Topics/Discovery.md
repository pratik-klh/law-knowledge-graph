# Discovery

**Discovery** = compulsory disclosure, at a party's request, of information relating to the litigation. It lets parties learn the facts and narrow the issues.

**Governing law:** FRCP **26–37** in federal court; state rules in state court (usually similar, but check for differences).
- [[FRCP Rule 26 - General Discovery Provisions|Rule 26]] is the foundation: timing, disclosures, scope, limits, privilege, protective orders.
- **Rule 37** enforces compliance.
