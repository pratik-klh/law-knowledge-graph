# In Personam Jurisdiction

Settles the **rights and obligations between persons**, based on the party's **presence** within the state's territory or **citizenship** in the state.

- One of the three types of PJ, alongside [[In Rem Jurisdiction]] and [[Quasi in Rem Jurisdiction]].
- Modern test: [[Minimum Contacts]] ([[International Shoe Co. v. Washington]]).
