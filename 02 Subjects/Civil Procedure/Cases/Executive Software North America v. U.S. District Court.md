# Executive Software North America, Inc. v. U.S. District Court (1994)

- **Majority:** supplemental jurisdiction is a **matter of right**. Courts may decline only on the grounds listed in §1367(c)(1)–(4).
- **Dissent:** supplemental jurisdiction is **always discretionary**.
- **Circuit split:** some circuits follow the Ninth Circuit; others follow the district court's approach in this case.
