# T.B. Harms Co. v. Eliscu (1964)

## Rule
When there is **no need to determine any question of federal law**, either its meaning or its application, there is **no FQJ**.

(Context: a dispute over ownership of copyrights governed by contract/state law, not the Copyright Act itself.)
