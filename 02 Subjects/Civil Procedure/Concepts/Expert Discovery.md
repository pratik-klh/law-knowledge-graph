# Expert Discovery (Rule 26(b)(4))

- Any party may **depose** an expert who has been disclosed to testify. If a report is required, the deposition happens only after the report.
- **Draft reports** are protected.
- **Attorney–expert communications** are protected, **except** those about compensation, facts or data the attorney provided that the expert considered, and assumptions the attorney provided.
- **Non-testifying consulting experts:** their opinions aren't discoverable, except for court-ordered exams ([[Physical and Mental Examinations|Rule 35]]) or exceptional circumstances where the facts can't practicably be obtained elsewhere.
- The party seeking expert discovery **pays the expert** a reasonable fee for time spent responding.
