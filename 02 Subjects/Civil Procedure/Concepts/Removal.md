# Removal

Gives the **defendant**, already sued in a court of competent jurisdiction, the right to choose its own forum by moving a state case to federal court.

**Statutes:** [[28 USC 1441 - Removal]] · [[28 USC 1446-1447 - Removal Procedure]]

## Primary requirement
The federal court must have had **original jurisdiction** if the case had been filed there first.

## Additional requirements
- **All defendants** must agree to removal.
- Only **original defendants** can remove ([[Shamrock Oil & Gas Corp. v. Sheets]]).
- **Forum-defendant rule:** in a diversity case, no removal if any defendant is a citizen of the forum state (§1441(b)).
- §1441(f): federal courts aren't barred from hearing a case just because the state court lacked jurisdiction over it.

## Process (§§1446–1447)
- The **federal court** controls. D files a notice of removal in federal court and serves copies on the other parties and the state court clerk (§1446(d)).
- **Timing:** within **30 days** of learning the case is removable (usually service of the summons and complaint). **Diversity:** outside limit of **1 year** after commencement (§1446(c)).
- To challenge: **motion to remand** (§1447(c)).

## Test for removal
Could the action have been brought in federal court originally? If yes, it can be removed **unless**:
- It's a diversity case and a defendant is from the forum state
- Any defendant objects
- Removal is untimely (more than 30 days after service)

> [!example] Hypo
> P (NY) sues D1 (NJ) and D2 (NJ) in California Superior Court. Neither D has contact with California. Can Ds remove? **Yes, if the amount in controversy exceeds $75,000.** There's complete diversity and no D is from California. (Ds would also have a PJ objection.)
