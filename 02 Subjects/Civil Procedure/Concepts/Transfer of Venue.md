# Transfer of Venue

## §1406: improper venue ([[28 USC 1406 - Transfer for Improper Venue]])
- Judge can **dismiss without prejudice**, **or**
- **Transfer** in the interest of justice to a court where the case **could have been brought**.
- If the court has SMJ, the case can go to a court that has PJ over D and is proper under the venue rules.
- Up to the court's discretion, but judges usually transfer when possible.
- **Law applied:** the **transferee** court's law, as if the case had been filed there originally.

## §1404(a): convenience ([[28 USC 1404 - Transfer for Convenience]])
- Venue is proper, but a party wants to move. The court may transfer to a venue where the case **might have been brought** (PJ + venue satisfied), **or** to any venue all parties **consent** to.
- The movant must show the new venue is more convenient for parties and witnesses, and/or in the interest of justice.
- **Interest-of-justice facts:** access to witnesses · access to the forum · docket congestion · speed to trial
- **Law applied:** the **transferor** court's law. It's a change of courtrooms, not a change of law.

## Why the law rule differs
So parties can't manipulate the system by forum shopping and then transferring. See [[Klaxon Co. v. Stentor Electric Manufacturing|Klaxon]].
