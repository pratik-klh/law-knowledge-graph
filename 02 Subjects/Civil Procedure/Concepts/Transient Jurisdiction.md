# Transient ("Tag") Jurisdiction

- PJ based on serving D while D is **physically present** in the forum.
- [[Burnham v. Superior Court]]: still valid, even if D is in the state briefly or for reasons unrelated to the litigation.
- Constitutional if the presence is **intentional, voluntary, and knowing**.
- One of the [[Traditional Bases of Personal Jurisdiction]] and a form of [[General Jurisdiction]].
