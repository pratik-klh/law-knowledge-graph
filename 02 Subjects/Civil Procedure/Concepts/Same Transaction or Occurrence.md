# Same Transaction or Occurrence

Used for compulsory [[Counterclaims]] (13(a)), [[Crossclaims]] (13(g)), and [[Permissive Joinder of Parties]] (20(a)).

## Tests courts use
- Are the **issues of fact and law** largely the same?
- Will **substantially the same evidence** come up in both?
- Is there a **logical relationship** between the claim and the counterclaim? ([[LASA v. Alexander]])

Seems to be essentially the same test as [[Common Nucleus of Operative Fact]] for [[Supplemental Jurisdiction]].
