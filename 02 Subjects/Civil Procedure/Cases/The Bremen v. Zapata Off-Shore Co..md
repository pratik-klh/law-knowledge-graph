# The Bremen v. Zapata Off-Shore Co. (1972)

## Rule
[[Forum Selection Clauses]] are given effect unless "**unfair or unreasonable**."

## Exceptions argued by plaintiffs
Fraud or duress · forum changed since the agreement · inequality of bargaining power
