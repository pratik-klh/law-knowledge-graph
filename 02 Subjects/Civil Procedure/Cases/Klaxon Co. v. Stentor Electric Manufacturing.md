# Klaxon Co. v. Stentor Electric Manufacturing Co. (1941)

## Rule
Federal courts must apply the **conflict-of-law rules of the state in which they sit**.

## Significance: choice of state law
After deciding state law applies, you must also decide **which state's** law. Look to the conflict rules of the state where the federal court sits. (Adjusted after transfers; see [[Transfer of Venue]].)
