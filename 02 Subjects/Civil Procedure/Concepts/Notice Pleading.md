# Notice Pleading

- The FRCP emphasized **common sense, simplicity, and flexibility**, and replaced **fact pleading** with **notice pleading**.
- A complaint doesn't need to narrow down the plaintiff's allegations in detail. It's enough that D gets **fair notice** of P's claim: the event P is suing over, and why.
- **Rationale:** facts get developed during [[Discovery]].

## Cases
- [[Dioguardi v. Durning]]: Rule 8(a) is construed liberally
- [[Conley v. Gibson]]: "no set of facts"
- [[Erickson v. Pardus]]: "specific facts are not necessary"
- Tightened by the [[Plausibility Standard]] ([[Bell Atlantic Corp. v. Twombly|Twombly]], [[Ashcroft v. Iqbal|Iqbal]])
