# Mandatory Disclosures (Rule 26(a))

Three disclosures that require **no request**. All must be written, signed, and served.

## 1. Initial disclosures (26(a)(1))
Within **14 days** after the [[Discovery Planning and Timing|26(f) conference]], based on information reasonably available (supplement later):
- Names and contact info of people likely to have discoverable information the party **may use**, and the subjects of that information
- Copies or descriptions of **documents and tangible things** the party may use to support its claims or defenses
- **Computation of damages** by category, with supporting documents

## 2. Expert disclosures (26(a)(2))
- At least **90 days before trial**. Rebuttal-only experts: within **30 days** of the other side's disclosure.
- **Retained experts** must give a signed written report with six things:
  1. All opinions and their basis
  2. Facts and data considered
  3. Exhibits to be used
  4. Qualifications, including publications from the past **10 years**
  5. Cases testified in during the past **4 years**
  6. Compensation

## 3. Pretrial disclosures (26(a)(3))
- At least **30 days before trial**: witnesses (will call / may call / by deposition) and exhibits.
- The opposing party has **14 days** to object, or objections are waived (unless good cause).
