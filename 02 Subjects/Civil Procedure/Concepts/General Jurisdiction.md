# General Jurisdiction

D has such a degree of contacts with the forum that the forum can assert jurisdiction over D **for all matters**, even claims unrelated to the forum contacts.

## Bases
- Consent
- Presence + service ([[Transient Jurisdiction]])
- Residence
- Property
- **Systematic and continuous activity**

## Test (systematic and continuous activity)
1. How many contacts does D have with the forum to satisfy the basis? **And**
2. How "at home" is D, such that due process is satisfied?
   - **Individuals:** usually their **domicile**
   - **Corporations:** usually their **state of incorporation** or **principal place of business**

## Key case
[[Goodyear Dunlop Tires Operations v. Brown]]: GJ exists when a corporation's operations in a state are so substantial and continuous that a suit on dealings distinct from those activities is justified.
