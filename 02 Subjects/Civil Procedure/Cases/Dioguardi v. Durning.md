# Dioguardi v. Durning (1944)

## Rule
- [[FRCP Rule 8 - General Rules of Pleading|Rule 8(a)]] is construed **liberally**.
- The purpose of pleading under the FRCP is to give **notice** to D so D knows what the cause of action is.

(Context: an inartfully drafted pro se complaint by an importer against the customs collector survived.)
