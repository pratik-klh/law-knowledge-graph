# Notice and Service of Process

**Notice** is the constitutional requirement that any party whose rights may be affected by a legal action must be informed of it. It tells a person a lawsuit is happening and gives them a fair chance to respond.

## Types of service of process
- Personal delivery
- Service through the mail
- Service by publication
- [[Attachment]]

## Role in PJ
PJ = **notice (service) + a basis**. See [[Personal Jurisdiction]].

## Related defenses
Insufficient process and insufficient service of process are **waivable** [[FRCP Rule 12 - Defenses and Objections|Rule 12(b)]] defenses.
