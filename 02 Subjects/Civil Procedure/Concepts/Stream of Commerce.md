# Stream of Commerce

- A sovereignty factor: if D **knows** it is part of a **regular and substantial flow** of products into the forum, that supports [[Minimum Contacts]].
- Most justices in [[Asahi Metal Industry Co. v. Superior Court]] were persuaded by this "stream of commerce" rationale. It also helps to show [[Purposeful Availment]] (advertising, design, customer advice in the forum).
- [[Goodyear Dunlop Tires Operations v. Brown]]: [[Specific Jurisdiction]] can arise when a non-resident D, acting outside the forum, places a product in the stream of commerce that causes harm **within** the forum.
