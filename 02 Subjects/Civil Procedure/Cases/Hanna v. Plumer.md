# Hanna v. Plumer (1965)

## Bottom line
Whether a federal court must follow state practice depends on the **source** of the conflicting federal practice.

## Rule
When a **[[Federal Rules of Civil Procedure|Federal Rule of Civil Procedure]] covers the point in dispute**, follow the Federal Rule and skip the Erie / outcome-determinative analysis.

(Context: FRCP 4 service on a family member at home, versus a state in-hand service rule.)
