# Discovery Cheat Sheet

## Numbers and deadlines
| Item | Rule | Number |
|---|---|---|
| Initial disclosures | 26(a)(1) | 14 days after the 26(f) conference |
| Expert disclosures | 26(a)(2) | 90 days before trial; 30 days for rebuttal |
| Pretrial disclosures | 26(a)(3) | 30 days before trial; objections within 14 days |
| Earliest Rule 34 requests | 26(d) | 22 days after the complaint is served |
| Depositions per side | 30(a)(2) | 10 without leave |
| Deposition length | 30(d)(1) | 1 day of 7 hours |
| Re-deposing a witness | 30(a)(2) | Leave of court required |
| Interrogatories | 33(a) | 25, including discrete subparts |
| Interrogatory response | 33(b) | 30 days, under oath |
| Requests for admission | 36 | No set limit; 30 days; silence = admitted |
| Request for production response | 34(b) | 30 days |
| Unavailable-witness distance | 32(a)(4) | More than 100 miles |
| Written deposition recross objection | 32(d)(3)(C) | 7 days |
| Exam order | 35 | Court order + good cause + condition in controversy |

## Who can be asked what
| Tool | Directed at | Under oath? | Key limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Depositions\|Deposition (oral)]] | Any person (subpoena for non-parties) | Yes | 10 per side, 7 hours |
| [[Depositions\|Deposition (written)]] | Any person | Yes | Same leave rules |
| [[Interrogatories]] | Parties only | Yes | 25 |
| [[Requests for Production]] | Parties (subpoena for non-parties) | n/a | 30-day response |
| [[Physical and Mental Examinations\|Rule 35 exam]] | Party or person in the party's control | n/a | Court order, good cause |
| [[Requests for Admission]] | Parties | Not required | Deemed admitted if ignored |
