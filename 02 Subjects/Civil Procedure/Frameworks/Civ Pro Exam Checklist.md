# Civ Pro Exam Checklist

Work through a fact pattern in roughly this order. Each step links to its framework or hub.

## 1. Can this court hear the case? ([[Jurisdiction Triangle]])
- [ ] **PJ** over each defendant: [[PJ Analysis Framework]]
  - Notice and service · long-arm statute · GJ or SJ · minimum contacts (sovereignty) · fairness
- [ ] **SMJ** for each claim:
  - [[Diversity Jurisdiction]] (complete diversity + more than $75k) or [[Federal Question Jurisdiction]] (well-pleaded complaint + substantial)
  - Extra claims or parties: [[Supplemental Jurisdiction Analysis]]
- [ ] **Venue:** [[28 USC 1391 - Venue|§1391]]. Transfer? (§1404 / §1406) · [[Forum Non Conveniens]] · [[Forum Selection Clauses]]
- [ ] **Removal?** [[Removal]]: original jurisdiction, all Ds consent, forum-defendant rule, 30 days / 1 year

## 2. What law applies?
- [ ] State claim in federal court: [[Erie Analysis Framework]] (statute → FRCP → Byrd), then which state ([[Klaxon Co. v. Stentor Electric Manufacturing|Klaxon]])

## 3. Pleadings
- [ ] Complaint sufficient? Rule 8(a)(2) plus the [[Plausibility Standard]] (Rule 9(b) for fraud)
- [ ] D's response: Rule 12 motion or answer. **Waiver** of PJ, venue, process, and service under Rule 12(h)? ([[FRCP Rule 12 - Defenses and Objections]])
- [ ] Affirmative defenses pled? Amendment (Rule 15)? Rule 11?

## 4. Joinder (three buckets: PJ + SMJ + rule)
- [ ] Claims: Rule 18 · counterclaims (compulsory or permissive?) · crossclaims
- [ ] Parties: Rule 20 · Rule 19 (feasible? 19(b) factors) · Rule 14 (derivative liability?)
- [ ] §1367(b) diversity trap for P's claims against added parties

## 5. Discovery
- [ ] Is the information within scope and proportional? Privileged? Work product? Protective order? → [[Discovery Cheat Sheet]]

## 6. Summary judgment
- [ ] Who moves? Who bears the burden at trial? → [[Summary Judgment Analysis]]
