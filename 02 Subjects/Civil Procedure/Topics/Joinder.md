# Joinder

> [!important] Three buckets for **every** claim
> Joinder rules **don't** provide jurisdiction. Read each rule as starting with "assuming jurisdiction exists." For each claim, check:
> 1. [[Personal Jurisdiction]]?
> 2. [[Subject Matter Jurisdiction]] (often through [[Supplemental Jurisdiction]])?
> 3. Are the joinder rules satisfied?

## 4 categories of claims
1. A **claim** by the plaintiff
2. A **[[Counterclaims|counterclaim]]** by the defendant against the plaintiff
3. A **[[Crossclaims|crossclaim]]** by one defendant against a co-defendant
4. A **third-party claim** by a defendant (as third-party plaintiff) against a third-party defendant ([[Impleader]])

## Joinder of claims
- [[Joinder of Claims]] (Rule 18): join as many claims as you have against an opposing party
- [[Counterclaims]] (Rule 13(a)/(b)) · [[Crossclaims]] (Rule 13(g))

## Joinder of parties
- [[Permissive Joinder of Parties]] (Rule 20)
- [[Required Joinder of Parties]] (Rule 19)
- [[Impleader]] (Rule 14)
