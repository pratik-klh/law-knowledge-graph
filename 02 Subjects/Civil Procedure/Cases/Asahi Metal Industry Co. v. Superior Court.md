# Asahi Metal Industry Co. v. Superior Court (1987)

## Significance
Gave the factors for both prongs of [[Minimum Contacts]].

### Sovereignty: [[Purposeful Availment]] factors
- Was the product designed for the forum?
- Was there advertising in the forum?
- Are there customer advice facilities in the forum?
- Is there a distribution arrangement with an agent in the forum?
- [[Stream of Commerce]]: if D knows it's part of a regular and substantial flow of products into the forum. *Most justices are persuaded by this rationale.*

### Fairness factors (no one factor is determinative)
- Forum state's interest in regulating the activity or providing a forum for its residents
- Relative convenience to the parties and whether there is an alternative forum
- Desire to avoid multiple lawsuits
- Any other factor bearing on fairness

See [[Fairness Factors]].
