# United Mine Workers v. Gibbs (1966)

## Rule: power
Federal and state claims that derive from a [[Common Nucleus of Operative Fact]] can be heard together. The court has power over the whole case.

## Rule: discretion (Gibbs factors)
Supplemental jurisdiction is **discretionary**. Consider:
- Does it increase **judicial economy**?
- Does it avoid needless decisions of **state law**? Are the federal claims dismissed before trial?
- Do **state issues predominate**?
- Are state issues closely tied to **federal policy**?
- Will there be **jury confusion** if tried together?

Codified (in modified form) in [[28 USC 1367 - Supplemental Jurisdiction]]. See [[Supplemental Jurisdiction]].
