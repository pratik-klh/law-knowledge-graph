# Minimum Contacts

> "[D]ue process requires only that in order to subject a defendant to a judgment in personam, if he be not present within the territory of the forum, he have certain **minimum contacts** with it such that the maintenance of the suit does not offend **traditional notions of fair play and substantial justice**." [[International Shoe Co. v. Washington]]

## Two prongs
1. **Sovereignty prong:** are there minimum contacts between D and the forum? Examine the contacts. See [[Purposeful Availment]] and [[Stream of Commerce]].
2. **Fairness prong:** does the suit offend fair play and substantial justice? Examine the [[Fairness Factors]].

> [!important] Threshold question
> Minimum contacts is a **threshold** question. If there are no minimum contacts, fairness and convenience are irrelevant. ([[World-Wide Volkswagen Corp. v. Woodson]])

## Focus on the defendant
The focus is on **D's** conduct and connection with the forum, such that D "should reasonably anticipate being haled into court there," not on P's interests or the forum's interests. ([[World-Wide Volkswagen Corp. v. Woodson]])

## Categories of contact (International Shoe)
| Contacts | Cause of action | PJ? |
|---|---|---|
| Continuous and systematic | Arises from the contacts | **YES** |
| Casual presence | Unrelated | **NO** |
| Isolated activity | Arises from that activity | **MAYBE** ([[Specific Jurisdiction]]) |
