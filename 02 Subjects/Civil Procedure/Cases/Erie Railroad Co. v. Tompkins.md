# Erie Railroad Co. v. Tompkins (1938)

## Holding
Overruled [[Swift v. Tyson]]. There is no federal general common law.
- **Substantive** law is governed by **state** law.
- **Procedural** law is governed by **federal** law.

## Erie test
- **Federal issue** (e.g., interpreting a federal statute): substance and procedure are both federal.
- **Not a federal issue:** state law for **substantive** issues (e.g., negligence), federal law for **procedural** issues (e.g., number of days to answer).

## Significance
Foundation of the [[Erie Doctrine]]. Later refined by [[Guaranty Trust Co. v. York]], [[Byrd v. Blue Ridge Rural Electric Cooperative]], and [[Hanna v. Plumer]].
