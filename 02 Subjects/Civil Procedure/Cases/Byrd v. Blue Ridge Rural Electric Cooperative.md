# Byrd v. Blue Ridge Rural Electric Cooperative (1958)

## Rule: [[Byrd Balancing Test]]
Weigh (1) the strength of the **state** interest, (2) the strength of the **federal** interest, and (3) a **modified** outcome-determinative test (a *likely*, not merely *possible*, effect on outcome).

(Context: the federal interest in jury allocation of fact issues outweighed the state practice of having a judge decide.)
