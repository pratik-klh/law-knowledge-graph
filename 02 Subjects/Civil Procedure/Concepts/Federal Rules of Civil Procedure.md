# Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (FRCP)

- Within the federal system there is **one** set of rules for how civil cases move through federal court: the FRCP. (Found in the Friedenthal supplement.)
- Many states model their own court rules on the FRCP.
- Districts may have **local rules** and individual judges may have **practices**, but all must be consistent with the FRCP.
- The FRCP emphasized common sense, simplicity, and flexibility, and shifted pleading from fact pleading to [[Notice Pleading]].

## Rules to focus on
- [[FRCP Rule 8 - General Rules of Pleading|Rule 8(a)]]: what goes in a complaint
- [[FRCP Rule 8 - General Rules of Pleading|Rule 8(b)]]: what goes in an answer
- [[FRCP Rule 12 - Defenses and Objections|Rule 12(b)]]: bases for moving to dismiss
- [[Summary Judgment|Rule 56]]: how summary judgment motions work

## In Erie analysis
When a valid FRCP directly covers the issue, it controls over state law. See [[Hanna v. Plumer]] and [[Walker v. Armco Steel Corp.]]
