# Scope of Discovery

- Only **parties** can seek discovery, though it can be obtained **from non-parties** (by subpoena).
- Information **need not be admissible** to be discoverable (e.g., hearsay).
- Must be **reasonable**, weighing burden against benefit (**proportionality**): issues at stake, money at stake, accessibility, resources.
- The court may limit the number of depositions and interrogatories and the length of depositions. Court or local rules may limit requests for admission.
- **ESI** that isn't reasonably accessible because of undue burden or cost need not be produced, but the court may order it for good cause.
- The court may limit discovery that is: duplicative or cumulative, or obtainable more cheaply elsewhere; something the party already had ample opportunity to get; or outside the scope of Rule 26(b).
