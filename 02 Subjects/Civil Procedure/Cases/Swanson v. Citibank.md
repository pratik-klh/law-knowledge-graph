# Swanson v. Citibank, N.A. (2010)

## Discrimination claim: should **not** have been dismissed
> "Swanson's complaint identifies the type of discrimination she thinks occurred, by whom, and when. That is all she needed to put in the complaint."

Meets [[FRCP Rule 8 - General Rules of Pleading|Rule 8(a)(2)]] under the [[Plausibility Standard]].

## Fraud claim: **properly** dismissed
- Theory: Ds committed fraud by announcing plans to make loans when the real intent was to exclude African Americans.
- Fraud must meet the higher standard of [[FRCP Rule 9(b) - Pleading Fraud|Rule 9(b)]]: the false statement · what it was about · **detrimental reliance** (how P relied, what harm resulted).

See [[Rule 12(b)(6) Motion to Dismiss]]
