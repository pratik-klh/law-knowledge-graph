# Burden of Production and Persuasion

**Proof** consists of two burdens:

| Burden | Meaning | Usually on |
|---|---|---|
| **Production** | Produce a minimum amount of evidence supporting **each element** of the cause of action | P (D for [[Affirmative Defenses]]) |
| **Persuasion** | Convince the fact-finder, by the required standard of proof, that your evidence outweighs the other side's | P (preponderance in civil cases; see [[Civil vs Criminal Cases]]) |

## Why it matters at summary judgment
- If you **bear** the burden, you need a preponderance on **every** element.
- If you **don't**, you only have to show the other side **can't win on one element**.
- So SJ is easier to get on an issue where you **don't** bear the burden. See [[Summary Judgment Analysis]].
