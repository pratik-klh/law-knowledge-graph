# 28 U.S.C. §1404(a): Transfer for Convenience

- Venue is proper, but transfer is sought for the **convenience of parties and witnesses** and in the **interest of justice**.
- Transfer to a district where the case **might have been brought**, or to one all parties consent to.
- The **original (transferor) court's law** applies.
