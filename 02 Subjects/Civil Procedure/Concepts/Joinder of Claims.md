# Joinder of Claims (Rule 18(a))

> "A party asserting a claim, counterclaim, crossclaim, or third-party claim may join, as independent or alternate claims, **as many claims as it has** against an opposing party."

- Claims don't have to be related.
- But there **must still be SMJ and PJ for each claim** (see the three buckets in [[Joinder]]).
- If unrelated claims would confuse the trial, see [[Severance and Separate Trials]].
