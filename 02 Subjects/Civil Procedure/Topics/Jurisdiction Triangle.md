# Jurisdiction Triangle

A case must clear all three before a court can hear it:

| Side | Question |
|---|---|
| [[Personal Jurisdiction]] (PJ) | Does the court have power over **this defendant**, so it can enter and enforce a judgment against them? |
| [[Subject Matter Jurisdiction]] (SMJ) | Is this court empowered to hear **this kind of case**? |
| [[Venue]] (V) | Is this the right **district** (geographic location) within the court system? |

## Venue compared to PJ and SMJ
![[Venue Compared to PJ and SMJ.png]]

- **Like PJ:** venue can be **waived**. If a party does not raise venue in a timely manner, it cannot raise it later to get the case out of federal court. (See waiver under [[FRCP Rule 12 - Defenses and Objections|Rule 12(h)]].)
- **Like SMJ:** the court can raise venue *sua sponte*, and can change venue to correct it or in the interests of justice. Venue is **statutory**: statutes set where a suit can be filed and when a court can transfer.

## Joinder reminder
For **every** claim, check three separate buckets: PJ, SMJ, and the joinder rules. See [[Joinder]].
