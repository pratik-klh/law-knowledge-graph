# Ashcroft v. Iqbal (2009)

## Rule
- To survive a motion to dismiss, a complaint must contain sufficient factual matter, accepted as true, "to state a claim to relief that is **plausible on its face**."
- **Facial plausibility:** facts allow the court to draw the reasonable inference that D is liable.
- Not a probability requirement, but more than a sheer possibility.
- Detailed factual allegations aren't required, but more than **naked assertions, labels, and conclusions** is.

## Significance
Extended [[Bell Atlantic Corp. v. Twombly|Twombly]] to **all** civil actions. See [[Plausibility Standard]] · [[Rule 12(b)(6) Motion to Dismiss]]
