# Jeub v. B/G Foods, Inc. (1942)

## Rules
- Rule 14's "who is or may be liable" language only provides **procedure**. It does **not** create substantive rights.
- A court can always **stay** execution of a judgment against a third party until the original judgment is paid or satisfied.
- **Impleader requires derivative liability.** Arising from the same transaction or occurrence isn't enough.

(Context: a restaurant sued over contaminated ham impleaded the meat supplier, Swift & Co.)
