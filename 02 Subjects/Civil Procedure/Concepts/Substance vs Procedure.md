# Substance vs Procedure

- **Substance** = the rights and duties that regulate everyday relationships (e.g., negligence law, contract law).
- **Procedure** = the process courts use to resolve disputes about those substantive rights and duties.

## Why it matters later
The line between the two drives the [[Erie Doctrine]]. In diversity and supplemental-jurisdiction cases, federal courts apply **state substantive law** and **federal procedural law**. Tests for drawing the line:
- [[Outcome Determinative Test]] ([[Guaranty Trust Co. v. York]])
- [[Byrd Balancing Test]] ([[Byrd v. Blue Ridge Rural Electric Cooperative]])
- [[Hanna v. Plumer]]: if a valid FRCP covers the point, apply the FRCP
