# Severance and Separate Trials

The court can always **sever** claims into different lawsuits or order **separate trials**.

- **Rule 21:** "Misjoinder is not a ground for dismissing an action. On motion or on its own, the court may at any time, on just terms, add or drop a party. The court may also sever any claim against a party."
- **Rule 42(b):** "For convenience, to avoid prejudice, or to expedite and economize, the court may order a separate trial of one or more separate issues, claims, crossclaims, counterclaims, or third-party claims."
