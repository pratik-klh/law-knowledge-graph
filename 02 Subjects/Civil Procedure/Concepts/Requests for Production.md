# Requests for Production (Rule 34): parties only

- Covers **documents, ESI, and tangible things** in the party's possession, custody, or control, plus **entry onto land** to inspect, measure, photograph, sample, or test.
- The request must describe items or categories with **reasonable particularity** and state the time, place, and manner of inspection.
- **Timing:** earliest **22 days** after the complaint is served. The response is due **30 days** after service (if served before the first discovery conference, 30 days after the conference). Parties can stipulate otherwise.

## Producing
- Produce documents **as kept in the usual course of business**, or **organized and labeled** to match the request categories.
- If no form is specified (or the form is objected to), produce ESI in the form ordinarily maintained or a reasonably usable form. ESI need not be produced in more than one form.
- Large productions can be done **in stages** with stated dates.

## Objections
Must state the grounds and whether materials are being withheld. Objections can be partial. No full privilege log is required for Rule 34 withholding, but give enough information to discuss the objection. The requester can **move to compel**.

## Non-parties
Need a **subpoena**. A **subpoena duces tecum** requires appearing and bringing documents or things (common at depositions).
