# Civil vs Criminal Cases

|  | Civil | Criminal |
|---|---|---|
| **Parties** | Two (or more) private parties | The government sues to protect "the people" |
| **Results** | Liable or not liable. Remedies are financial compensation or injunctions | Guilty or not guilty. Punishments include jail, prison, probation, or fines |
| **Burden of proof** | Preponderance of the evidence (more likely than not) | Beyond a reasonable doubt (higher burden) |

- Both civil and criminal cases can be brought in federal **or** state court.
- See also: [[Burden of Production and Persuasion]] (how the civil burden plays out at [[Summary Judgment]]).
